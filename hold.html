Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about holding properties

What happens if I don't complete the purchase?

+

If you decide not to proceed with the purchase within your hold period, your deposit is fully refunded. There are no penalties or fees.

Can I extend my hold period?

+

Yes, you can extend your hold period by upgrading to a longer hold plan. The difference in deposit will be applied.

Is the deposit applied to the purchase price?

+

Yes, if you proceed with the purchase, your hold deposit is fully credited toward the purchase price.

What if someone else wants the same property?

+

Once you place a hold, the property is reserved exclusively for you during your hold period. No other buyers can place a hold on the same property.
